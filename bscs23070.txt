    -Transfer Learning from COCO: Started from yolov5s.pt pretrained on COCO instead of training from scratch. The early convolutional layers already knew general edge and texture filters, 
     which helped learn cell shapes faster and prevented overfitting on the single WBC class.

    -AutoAnchor Cluster Matching: Before training, AutoAnchor checked the dataset box sizes against default anchors. Best possible recall (BPR) was over 99% across the small (P3), medium  
    (P4), and large (P5) heads, so the anchors fit cell shapes well.

     from 0.8536 to 0.8550 without retraining or slowing down inference (10.4 ms per image).
